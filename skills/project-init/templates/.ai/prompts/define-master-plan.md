# Define the Master Plan

Use `project-init` to draft or revise product direction in `MASTER_PLAN.md`.

Record the product goal, users, outcomes, scope, non-goals, proposed features,
user-flow references, technical unknowns, assumptions, and open questions.
Keep user-provided details distinct from recommendations and inferences.

For a new project, use only the user's stated idea and confirmed answers. Put
the idea in the product goal, mark mentioned feature possibilities as
`Proposed`, leave unknown choices open, and do not invent screens, entities,
APIs, frameworks, tables, or tasks. Show the draft and obtain approval before
creating the missing file; keep an existing source of truth intact unless its
revision is separately approved.

Preserve existing decisions and ask for approval before revising an existing
file. Do not choose frameworks, tables, APIs, or implementation tasks here.
