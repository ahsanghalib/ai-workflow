# Lead qualification

Qualification is evidence-based, not score-based.

## Prospect kind and relationship context

The `Prospect Type` field describes only the record kind:

- `company`
- `individual`

Do not encode commercial or relationship roles in that field. Record flexible,
evidence-backed context in `Relationship Context`, `Why Relevant`, or `Notes`,
such as `buyer`, `hiring_contact`, `referrer`, `partner`, `peer`, `candidate`,
or another role that fits the observed situation. These labels are descriptive,
not proof of authority, intent, budget, or qualification.

## Commercial evidence

Record exact evidence for any lead transition. Useful dimensions include:

- problem/project is real rather than hypothetical;
- problem fits the user's software/AI integration capabilities;
- timing or next step exists;
- person has decision authority, influence, or a route to the decision-maker;
- budget/procurement context is known, when available.

Do not require every dimension before calling something a lead, but do not fill
unknown dimensions with guesses.

## Stages

- `none` — professional relationship only.
- `lead` — plausible commercial need/fit exists.
- `qualified` — specific need plus credible next step and sufficient context to
  justify active pursuit.
- `opportunity` — concrete project/business discussion is underway.
- `proposal` — proposal/quote/scope has been provided.
- `won` / `lost` — outcome is known.
