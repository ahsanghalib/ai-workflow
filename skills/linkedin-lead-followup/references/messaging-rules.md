# Follow-up messaging rules

## Grounding

Every message must be based on the actual thread, post, shared topic, referral,
or explicit prospect statement. Do not pretend to have read material that was
not inspected.

## Tone

- concise and professional;
- specific to the conversation;
- no generic praise or fake familiarity;
- no long capability dump;
- no pressure language;
- no fabricated urgency.

Use `humanizer` when available after the factual content is fixed. Humanization
must not introduce claims or personal history.

## Sequence

Prefer the least intrusive step that advances a real conversation:

1. useful public reply when the discussion is still public;
2. connection when context is established;
3. DM when there is a natural reason to move private;
4. discovery/call only when the conversation supports it.

Do not send an immediate services pitch simply because a connection was accepted.

## Follow-up after silence

Silence is not a hidden buying signal. Do not create an automated chase sequence.
A single context-specific follow-up may be reasonable when there was an actual
open question, promised next step, or time-sensitive dependency; otherwise leave
the relationship inactive.
