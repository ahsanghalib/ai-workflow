# Carousel narrative frameworks

Choose the shortest structure that preserves the approved post's argument.
Page count is an editorial decision; a five-page or seven-page format is not a
guarantee of performance or a LinkedIn requirement.

## Five-page outline

1. **Cover and promise** — name the specific problem, decision, or insight.
2. **Context or failed default** — explain why the reader should care.
3. **Core insight or mechanism** — show what is actually happening.
4. **Framework, example, or steps** — make the idea usable without adding
   unsupported claims.
5. **Takeaway and limitation** — state the bounded conclusion, sources or
   disclosure when needed, and only the approved next action.

Use five pages when the post has one clean mechanism or one practical lesson.

## Seven-page outline

1. **Cover and promise**.
2. **Why the reader should care**.
3. **Problem or failed default**.
4. **Evidence or mechanism**.
5. **Framework or steps, part one**.
6. **Framework or steps, part two or example**.
7. **Takeaway, limitation, sources, or approved CTA**.

Use seven pages only when the additional pages introduce necessary reasoning,
steps, or a concrete example. Do not split one sentence across pages merely to
reach seven.

## Page-to-claim mapping

Every page should have one job and an entry in the source-to-slide ledger:

<!-- markdownlint-disable MD013 MD060 -->

| Page | Job | Source claim or user-approved wording | Caveat/disclosure | Visual evidence |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | promise | C1 or approved framing | ... | supplied asset or none |

<!-- markdownlint-enable MD013 MD060 -->

If a page cannot be mapped to the approved post, evidence ledger, or supplied
asset, remove it or return it for content review.

## Anti-filler checks

- Combine pages that repeat the same claim or takeaway.
- Do not invent statistics, screenshots, testimonials, diagrams of unverified
  systems, or decorative “evidence.”
- Do not move a limitation only to a hidden final page when it changes how the
  earlier claim should be read.
- Keep the promise, mechanism, example, and conclusion consistent with the
  approved post; if the visual version changes the claim, return to post review.
