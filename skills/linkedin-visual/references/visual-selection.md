# Visual selection

The purpose of a visual is to make the idea easier to understand or remember,
not to decorate every post.

## Choose `none`

Prefer text-only when the post is:

- a short opinion/counterpoint whose force comes from the wording;
- a personal/build lesson without a useful diagram;
- a concise observation that would become weaker when padded into slides;
- already easy to scan and not dependent on structure or comparison.

## Choose `image`

Use one focal image when one visual model is enough, for example:

- a system/architecture diagram;
- one comparison or decision framework;
- one flow with a few nodes;
- one technical checklist that remains readable on a phone.

Avoid generic futuristic AI imagery, decorative robots, fake dashboards, and
stock-like illustrations that add no information.

## Choose `document`

Use a multi-page document when the post contains a sequence the reader benefits
from stepping through, such as:

- mechanism -> failure mode -> fix;
- comparison with several dimensions;
- architecture/system explanation;
- 4-6 checks or steps that need space;
- evidence/caveats that should remain visible rather than compressed.

Do not create filler pages to satisfy a preset page count.
