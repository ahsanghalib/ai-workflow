# Copywriting platform adapters

Use these small adapters after the general copywriting pass. The parent skill
still owns the platform workflow, approvals, evidence, and external actions.
Use the same boundary for every channel: adapt pacing, format, and reader
expectations without changing the approved claim, certainty, audience, or
action. Current limits, ad policies, disclosures, and publishing mechanics
belong to a current platform or compliance workflow.

## LinkedIn post

- Make the first two or three visible lines identify a specific reader,
  tension, observation, or useful promise.
- Keep one primary angle and one core takeaway. Prefer a mechanism, decision,
  example, or limitation over a generic trend summary.
- Use short paragraphs for scanning, but do not force every post into a viral
  template or fixed word/hashtag count.
- End with a bounded implication, honest question, or approved handoff. A CTA
  is optional.

## LinkedIn comment

- Start from the exact post and add one specific observation, distinction,
  useful extension, or natural question.
- Do not restate the post, flatter the author generically, pitch services, or
  imply a relationship that is not present.
- Keep it short enough to be a comment; normally 15–45 words unless precision
  genuinely requires more.

## X or other short social post

- Make one point legible without relying on a thread, image, or reply to supply
  the missing premise.
- Prefer a concrete observation, contrast, or decision over a compressed list of
  claims.
- Treat character limits and current ranking behavior as volatile platform
  facts, not permanent copywriting rules.

## Instagram or Facebook caption

- Let the caption add context, a useful detail, or a decision to the visual;
  do not imply that an image proves a result it cannot show.
- Put the reader, situation, and next step near the beginning when the caption
  is likely to be truncated.
- Use a call to action only when the parent workflow supplies a real action and
  the account is authorized to request it.

## Reddit post or reply

- Answer the question or contribute a specific experience before mentioning an
  offer, product, or link.
- Disclose relevant affiliation and avoid pretending to be an independent
  user.
- Respect the community's context; a technically correct sales pitch can still
  be irrelevant or unwelcome.

## TikTok, YouTube Short, or video script

- Write the spoken opening and on-screen text so the premise is understandable
  without manufactured shock or a bait-and-switch.
- Give the viewer a concrete example, demonstration, or reasoning step before
  the CTA.
- Keep the caption, title, thumbnail text, and spoken claim aligned; a stronger
  title must not overstate what the video proves.

## Blog post or newsletter

- State the reader's question, situation, or decision before expanding into
  background.
- Use headings, examples, transitions, and limitations to make the argument
  navigable; do not pad a short answer into a long article.
- Separate editorial explanation from current research, SEO, legal, or subject-
  matter review owned by another workflow.

## Advertising

- Make the promise, mechanism or deliverable, proof, qualification, and next
  step mutually consistent across the ad and its destination.
- Match the claim to the supplied audience, offer, price, availability, and
  evidence. Never create a result, guarantee, countdown, testimonial, or
  scarcity device to improve response.
- Treat legal, regulatory, platform-policy, disclosure, and accessibility
  review as separate gates before publication.

## Warm reply or follow-up

- Name the real context, answer or advance one open point, and ask at most one
  useful next question.
- Use a low-pressure next step. Connection acceptance, a like, or silence is
  not evidence of buying intent.
- Never turn a context-specific reply into a generic capability paragraph.

## Visual or carousel copy

- Preserve the approved source's claim, certainty, attribution, and caveats.
- Put one useful idea in each visual unit and shorten wording only to improve
  scanability; do not add a new thesis or unsupported visual implication.

## Long-form page, email, or proposal

- Make the argument and reader decision clear before polishing sentences.
- Use examples, proof, limitations, and transitions where they carry reasoning.
- Keep commercial language proportionate to the reader's context and the
  evidence available.
