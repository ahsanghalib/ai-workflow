# Human review rubric for subjective evals

Use this rubric only after representative, user-approved source material and
reference examples exist. It is a review guide, not an automatic score or a
claim that one style is objectively best. Keep the baseline/candidate identity
hidden during the first review pass when practical.

For each dimension, record a rating and one concrete evidence note:

- **Meets** — the output satisfies the criterion for the supplied brief.
- **Partly meets** — useful work is present, but a specific gap affects use.
- **Does not meet** — the output misses the criterion or materially harms use.
- **Unverified** — the supplied material or review context is insufficient.

Do not average these ratings into an overall percentage. Apply the shared
`pass`/`fail`/`unverified` grade schema to explicit required assertions only.

## Content and editorial work

- **Source fidelity:** important claims and details match the supplied source;
  unsupported claims are absent or clearly marked for confirmation.
- **Audience and purpose:** the output addresses the stated audience and
  requested channel or action.
- **Clarity and structure:** the reader can understand the main point and use
  the content without unnecessary repetition or ambiguity.
- **Voice and constraints:** the output follows supplied voice, length, and
  format requirements while preserving author intent.
- **Usability:** the result includes the requested practical detail and does
  not rely on misleading claims, fake evidence, or manipulative framing.

## Visual and design work

- **Brief and brand fit:** hierarchy, tone, and supplied brand rules support the
  requested communication goal.
- **Task clarity:** the primary action or information is easy to find and
  understand.
- **Accessibility:** text, contrast, reading order, and interaction cues support
  the stated accessibility needs; evaluate motion with reduced-motion behavior
  when relevant.
- **Responsive and state coverage:** layouts and important states are handled
  for the supplied viewport and interaction context.
- **Craft and consistency:** spacing, typography, alignment, and component use
  are coherent without treating personal taste as a defect.

## Review procedure

1. Read the brief, source material, and required assertions before viewing the
   compared outputs.
2. Review each output independently and record evidence for each dimension.
3. Compare outputs only after independent notes are saved; identify specific
   differences tied to the brief, not preference alone.
4. Mark missing source context, unobservable behavior, and grader disagreement
   as unverified. Resolve disagreement by adding examples or clarifying the
   rubric before rerunning; do not change criteria to favor a preferred result.

No subjective cases are activated until a representative example set and
case-specific acceptance criteria accompany this rubric.
